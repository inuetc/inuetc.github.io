About

Who is Inu Etc?

I'm Inu Etc, the founder of Inuidea.com and Inu Etc Digital Solutions, and the co-founder of Raw Travel Project and Zillion Media.

I'm a writer, blogger, web developer, creator, online business consultant, marketer, and traveler.

As a generalist, I combine a wide range of skills, from website design and development to course creation, social media management, content creation, and digital marketing.

With all these skills, I can be the all-in-one solution for your online business needs!

I like traveling, I'm a digital nomad, and I want to travel the world. In the upcoming years, I'd like to build a team and produce high-quality content while traveling the world.

I started freelancing in 2018, and founded Inuidea.com in 2019. I want to build more online businesses in the future so that I can work from anywhere and earn money to travel the world.

Passionate about exploration, I'm not only a seasoned traveler but also an aspiring mountaineer. In 2023, I successfully completed the Basic Mountaineering Course at the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Institute of Mountaineering and Allied Sports in Manali.

In everything I do, whether it's building businesses, crafting content, or exploring new places, my goal is simple: create a life of freedom, creativity, and impact.

I believe in working smart, earning passively, and doing what I love.

If you're someone who dreams of turning your skills into an online business, building a personal brand, or simply living life on your own terms, I'm here to help you make that happen.
