Who is Inu Etc?

I'm Inu Etc, the founder of Inuidea.com and Inu Etc Digital Solutions, and the co-founder of Raw Travel Project and Zillion Media. I'm a writer, blogger, web developer, creator, online business consultant, marketer, and traveler.

As a generalist, I combine a wide range of skills — from website design and development to course creation, social media management, content creation, and digital marketing. I started freelancing in 2018 and founded Inuidea.com in 2019.

I'm a digital nomad and an aspiring mountaineer — in 2023 I completed the Basic Mountaineering Course at ABVIMAS, Manali. In everything I do, my goal is simple: create a life of freedom, creativity, and impact.
